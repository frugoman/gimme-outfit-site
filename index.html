Your closet, styled daily

Get dressed in ten seconds.

Gimme Outfit turns photos of your own clothes into a digital closet, then suggests a look every morning for the weather, your calendar and the vibe you’re going for. Everything is private and runs on your iPhone.

What it does

01

A closet from your photos

Snap your clothes and Gimme Outfit removes the background and tags each piece automatically.

02

A daily look, with a Fit score

An outfit for today’s weather and plans. Shuffle it, lock the pieces you love, swap the rest.

03

Ask in plain words

Describe what you need and get a look back — or talk it through with the stylist chat.

04

Saved looks & outfit builder

Keep the combinations you like and put new ones together yourself.

05

Diary, planner & trips

Log what you wore, plan ahead, and get packing lists for your trips.

06

Wardrobe insights & wishlist

See how you actually use your closet, and ask “Will it work?” before you buy something new.

07

Widgets, mornings & Siri

Home and Lock Screen widgets, a morning notification with your look, and Siri shortcuts.

Private by design

Your closet never leaves your hands.

All of the intelligence runs on your device. There’s no Gimme Outfit account and no Gimme Outfit server — your photos, closet, calendar and location are never sent to us. If you use iCloud, your closet syncs through your own private iCloud.
